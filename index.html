The desk

Drawn the day it happens.

INKWIRE is a one-person cartoon desk. Every drawing is made by hand, in ink, on the day the story breaks — no committee, no house style, no press office to please. If a week passes without a cartoon worth keeping, the week passes.

Drawings are free to republish with a credit line. The archive is permanent, so a cartoon about an election still reads years later as a record of how the argument sounded at the time.
